# Phil (Bartender) — VA Script
**Character:** Phil, Bartender, Broken Hills — arm-wrestle commentary only (`phil` tags live in `hcfranci.msg`)

**Total recordable lines:** 17 (`phil1`–`phil17`)

---

> **Direction:** A ghoul bartender playing hype-man for the crowd during Francis's arm-wrestling matches. Not a fighter — a heckling, pun-drunk spectator having the time of his life. Lean into the anatomy puns (ARMs, WRISTing, HUMERUS, clavicle) as groan-worthy bar humor, delivered loud and gleeful like he's had a few. These are all one-off shouted lines, not a conversation — no need for a consistent scene arc, just enthusiastic ringside commentary.
>
> Note: these lines exist inside Francis's own dialog file (`hcfranci.msg`) because the original script pulls Phil's commentary text from there, but they are **Phil's voice, not Francis's** — a different actor/read from the `franci` tags in the same file.

---
