# Chuck and Buck Dunton — VA Script
**Character:** Chuck and Buck Dunton, identical twin brothers running the Dunton drymeat/brahmin operation in Klamath

**Total recordable lines:** 81 (`buck1`–`buck81`)

---

> **Direction:** Two adult male identical twins, heavily muscled bar-room bullies — think dumb muscle playing at being tough guys. One voice covers both; the game itself can't tell them apart half the time ("You see either Chuck or Buck Dunton, they both look so much alike that you're not sure which one you're looking at"), so don't try to differentiate them vocally. Default posture is a sneering, threatening bully looking for an excuse to swing — but it's a thin shell: get on their good side (help with their brahmin-rustling scheme, or beat one at arm-wrestling) and the same voice turns gruff, backslapping, "business partner" friendly. Humor is lowbrow and gross (drymeat "recipe" jokes, mocking a slow-witted PC). When actually cornered — caught running the rustling scam, confronted about the "Bug Men" costumes — the bravado cracks fast into fidgety, blustering backpedaling. Play the arc as bully → business partner → cornered coward, same guy throughout.
