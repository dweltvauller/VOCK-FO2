# Brother Paul — Voice Actor Script
**Character:** Brother Paul, computer monk of the Abbey
**Total recordable lines:** 26 (PAUL1–PAUL26)

> **Direction:** A computer monk from the Abbey with the vibe of an overworked, slightly anxious IT/computer geek. Intelligent, meticulous, and polite, but carrying a hint of exhaustion from routine data entry — and a secret, nerdy passion for botany he tries hard to keep on the down-low, which surfaces as touches of nervous excitement whenever plants come up.
