# Maida Buckner — VA Script
**Character:** Maida Buckner, Ardin's daughter — runs trading and drinks at Buckner House, Klamath

**Total recordable lines:** 49 (`maida1`–`maida49`)

---

> **Direction:** A tough, plain-spoken young woman in her twenties, hardened by running a
> struggling family business. Her default with strangers — especially tribals — is a scowl and a
> blunt, prejudiced edge; she doesn't hide her distaste and expects trouble. That guardedness melts
> as the PC proves useful: grudging respect once they trade fairly or help with Sulik's situation,
> real warmth and gratitude once they find her missing brother Torr. If genuinely angered, she goes
> cold and dismissive rather than loud. Play the arc as guarded/prejudiced → businesslike →
> genuinely relieved and grateful.
