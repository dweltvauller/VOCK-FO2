# AHS-7 — VA Script
**Character:** AHS-7, second-in-command of the Hubologist compound, San Francisco

**Total recordable lines:** 41 (`ahs71`–`ahs741`)

---

> **Direction:** AHS-7 is the second-in-command of the Hubologist enclave — a true believer, fanatically devoted to "the Hub," "the Great Wheel," and the pseudo-mystical jargon of his cult ("neurodynes," "alignment," "zeta scan," "AHS" ranks). He's smooth and unctuous when recruiting, condescending and faintly creepy when talking down to the "unenlightened" or a low-intelligence visitor ("poor child," "raw meat"), and turns cold and threatening in an instant when crossed or defied — full cult-leader menace, not cartoonish. Vocal qualities: measured, almost hypnotic warmth for the recruitment pitch and cosmology talk; oily, superior condescension for the low-INT branch; sudden hard edge with no warm-up when he calls the guards or rejects someone permanently. He should sound like a man utterly convinced of his own enlightenment — sincere fanaticism, not a con artist playing a part.
