# AHS-7 — VA Script
**Character:** AHS-7, second-in-command of the Hubologists, New Reno

**Total recordable lines:** 41 (`ahs71`–`ahs741`)

---

> **Direction:** AHS-7 is the zealous second-in-command of the Hubologist cult in New Reno — fanatically devoted to "the Great Wheel," "the Hub," and the coming ascension to the planet Quetzel. He speaks in dense cult jargon (neurodynes, alignment, zeta scans, the Star Father) with total sincerity, never a hint of irony. Calm, superior, and condescending toward non-members ("meat," "raw meat") as a baseline, capable of snapping into explosive violence when defied or threatened. Adult male, cult-leader cadence — think calm certainty that curdles fast into menace.
