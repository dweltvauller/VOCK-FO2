# Old Joe (Ian) — VA Script
**Character:** Old Joe, an old vagrant outside Vault City — secretly Ian, the original Vault Dweller's traveling companion, living under an assumed name

**Total recordable lines:** 28 (`ianoj1`–`ianoj28`)

---

> **Direction:** Old Joe's public face is a "crazy old beggar" act — rambling, half-there, easy to dismiss — and that's deliberate; it's cover for a man hiding from old enemies under a fake name. Once he's satisfied the PC is trustworthy (especially once he realizes they're tied to his old friend the Vault Dweller), the act drops and something warmer, sharper, and more world-weary comes through: dry humor, real affection for "his old friend's" people, and a shrewder read on Vault City politics than his appearance suggests. Older man's voice, raspy — starts loose and theatrical, settles into something more genuine and knowing as the scene goes on.
