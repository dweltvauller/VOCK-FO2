# Kurisu — VA Script
**Character:** Kurisu, Sulik's sister, captive at the Slaver's Camp / later Primitive Tribe village

**Total recordable lines:** 29 (`kur1`–`kur29`)

---

> **Direction:** Kurisu is a young tribal woman, Sulik's sister, held captive by slavers when the PC first meets her. She speaks in the same tribal patois as Sulik — dropped articles, "be" in place of "is," rhythmic and earthy phrasing ("Bonespirits say you be leading Sulik," "It be safe yet?"). Early on she's frightened but sharp, quick to size up the PC and plead her case with urgency rather than helplessness. Once free and headed home, she warms considerably — grateful, a little wondering, prone to talk about "the spirits" having plans for people. If the PC brings her brother home safely, she's overjoyed and tender toward him; if Sulik has died, she breaks down into quiet grief mid-line. The floats are shorter beats: a defiant hex at hostile players, a frightened plea during combat, and a trio of awed, half-joking lines if she learns the PC saved the world. Female voice, tribal/earthy inflection matching Sulik's established dialect, no particular real-world accent.

---

## Slaver Camp — Sulik Recognizes Her
*Sulik is in the party and slavers are still holding the camp.*

`kur1:` Sulik! [She catches herself and begins to whisper.] Do the spirits be teasing, or is that really you, brother?

*A beat later, after a tense pause where a twig snaps outside and Sulik reaches for his weapon.*

`kur2:` [Sulik's sister turns to you.] Bonespirits say you be leading Sulik. Name's Kurisu, but now not the time for proper greetin'. Please, you needin' to get us out of cage and away from de wicked men ruled by spirits of lust. [She sneers and spits in the dirt.] Filthy dogs will not be laying hands on us again. [Upon hearing this, Sulik's eyes widen as his face contorts with rage. He reaches for his weapon.]

Be keeping me around for their wicked pleasure. Please, you needin' to get us out of cage and away from de evil men.

## Home at Last — Arrival at the Primitive Tribe
*Escorted safely home. This is her greeting the moment she and the PC arrive at her village.*

`kur11:` Praise the spirits, we be home at last! Thank you so much, fren.

*Asked how she was captured.*

`kur12:` Was trading in nearby village. Slavers came. Much bloodshed and chaos. Then, everything go dark. Awoke in packed cage reeking of shit. Soon moved to smaller cage, with ones they like to be keeping. Peeped many slaves come and go. Sad to see people be treated like cattle.

*Asked what she'll do now — Sulik is with the party.*

`kur13:` Slavers hurt me, but I far from broken. The spirits guided you to ensure my freedom. Clearly, the spirits be planning something big for me. Spirits got big plans for you, too. And brother Sulik. He will be much missed, but the spirits say his place is walking by-and-by with you. Hell, even Grampy Bone like you, so I know Sulik in good hands. You take care.

*Asked what she'll do now — Sulik is not with the party.*

`kur14:` Slavers hurt me, but I far from broken. The spirits guided you to ensure my freedom. Clearly, the spirits be planning something big for me. Soon, I think I be going to hunt down brother. Knowing Sulik, he prolly got in trouble, trying to come help me. Now, perhaps I be the one helping him.

*The PC mentions having seen a tattooed, bone-pierced man matching Sulik's description.*

`kur15:` That be Sulik alright! And Grampy Bone, too! Please, could you go bring Sulik home? I be missing that man so much.

*The PC tells her Sulik is dead.*

`kur16:` Sulik... dead? Truly, this be a dark day. I know he be with Grampy now, but Sulik... he will be much missed. [She looks away as tears fill her eyes.] Please... I need being alone now. Thank you... for bringing me home.
