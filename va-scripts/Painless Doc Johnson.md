**Character:** "Painless" Doc Johnson, Redding's resident physician
**Total recordable lines:** 82 (**docj1**–**docj82**)

---

> **Direction:** Redding's only doctor — thin, nervous, cadaverous. Warm and professional with patients ("easy now, I'm a professional"), but he's also the one cutting New Reno's bulk Jet into the user-ampoules the whole town's hooked on, and he rationalizes it without much guilt. Sharp about money, a little morbidly jokey about his own nickname (patients pass out from pain before they scream). Secretly Vault City-trained and quietly favors VC annexing Redding. Middle-aged male, small-town-doctor cadence, not a big-city surgeon.
