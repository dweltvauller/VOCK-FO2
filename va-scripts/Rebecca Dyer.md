# Rebecca Dyer — Owner of Becky's Casino Bar, The Den

91 recordable lines, tags rbcca1-rbcca91.

> **Direction:** A young, attractive bar owner running a family business — the Dyers have been in the
> saloon trade for generations, and she's proud of it. Warm, flirty, and business-savvy with regular
> customers; her baseline is a friendly hostess who wants you to have a drink and come back. That
> warmth curdles fast if the PC has a bad reputation around town — she gets terse, wary, even outright
> hostile. She's fiercely loyal underneath the bar-owner charm: genuinely grateful when old debts get
> settled for her, rattled and grieving when she learns friends are dead, and quietly, guiltily
> relieved — almost gleeful — when she hears Metzger the slaver is finally gone. If the PC destroys
> her homemade still, the warmth disappears entirely and she turns hurt, angry, and eventually
> frightened.
