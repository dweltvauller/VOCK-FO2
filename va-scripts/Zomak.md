# Zomak — Voice Actor Script
**Character:** Zomak "the Destroyer," guard to the Brain, Gecko
**Total recordable lines:** 21 (ZOMAK1–ZOMAK21)

---

> **Direction:** Zomak is a large, burly ghoul who serves as guard to "the Brain," a mutant superintelligence cult leader. He is fanatically devoted to the Brain and the concept of "Renewal" (a cult ritual). Zomak is not very bright — he is easily flustered when caught in a slip-up and tries to cover it badly. Vocal qualities: deep, gravelly ghoul voice with enthusiastic, almost child-like devotion when talking about Renewal or the Brain; bluster and low-key menace when turning the player away; comic embarrassment when he accidentally reveals the Brain's location. The catchphrase "Renew, comrade!" should feel like a cult salute — rote but sincere.

> Lines marked PC are for context only — do not record.

---

## Greetings

ZOMAK1: Renew, comrade! I am Zomak. Some call me Zomak the Destroyer. What is your business here?
*First meeting only. Proud self-introduction — he enjoys saying his own name.*

ZOMAK2: Renew, comrade! What are you doing here? State your business to Zomak the Destroyer.
*All repeat visits. More impatient, still name-drops himself.*

## Just Looking Around

ZOMAK3: You should look somewhere else, or Zomak will show you the stars.
*Dismissive threat. Delivered flatly — he's said this before.*

## I Wish to Renew

ZOMAK4: Then you must see him.
*Reverent. "Him" is spoken with quiet awe — the Brain is never named directly.*

ZOMAK5: He is the granter of enlightenment. It is he that promises Renewal to all who follow him.
*Recited like a creed. Sincere, a little vacant.*

ZOMAK6: Well, he's right back... Hey! Just who are you, anyway?
*Starts to point the way, catches himself, suddenly suspicious.*

ZOMAK7: Well, wander on out of here, then.
*Flat. No argument — just go. Plays if the PC claims to be "just a wanderer."*

ZOMAK9: Well, you should choose to leave. Get out of here.
*Player claims to be the Chosen One. Also used when the Brain is hostile/dead. Firm dismissal.*

ZOMAK8: Well, in that case... Come on in. He's right in the next room. Don't keep him waiting.
*Player gives an acceptable answer (here for annual renewal, or just has questions). Satisfied — waves them through warmly.*

## Low-INT Branch

ZOMAK10: Yes, you can all Renew inside.
*Mishears a low-INT grunt as a renewal request. Cheerfully waves them in.*

ZOMAK11: Ook? What do you mean by that? Are you dumb?
*Baffled. Genuine confusion — he's not sure if the player is messing with him.*

ZOMAK12: Get out of here. The Brain doesn't have time for the likes of you.
*Exasperated. Doesn't notice he just blurted out "the Brain."*

## I Took a Wrong Turn

ZOMAK13: There's nothing else down here but the Brain's lair. Boy, are you dumb.
*Condescending — then immediately realizes what he said.*

ZOMAK14: Oops. Uh, there's nothing here. Nothing at all. Just go away now.
*Panicked backpedal. Awkward, obvious lie — plays if the PC calls him on mentioning "the Brain."*

ZOMAK15: Yup. I'm sure. Now get out.
*Doubling down. Trying hard to sound casual and failing.*

ZOMAK16: Oh, heh, heh... Yeah. I knew that. Umm, well, er, did I pass?
*PC claims they were just testing him. Completely bought it. Flustered, hopeful, embarrassed — comic beat.*

ZOMAK17: Okay, then. You can go right in.
*Relieved. Both possible PC responses to ZOMAK16 lead here — he lets them in either way.*

## Returning Visitor With the Talisman

ZOMAK18: You have the sign. Go right in.
*Immediate recognition, respectful. No further questions.*

ZOMAK19: Yes, the sacred talisman of Renewal. Renew, comrade! Renew!
*Reverent excitement — the talisman is holy to him.*
