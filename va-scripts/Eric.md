# Eric — VA Script
**Character:** Eric, hceric, Broken Hills

**Total recordable lines:** 22 (`eric1`–`eric22`)

---

> **Direction:** Eric is a ghoul who attracts flies due to his biology and can't afford enough power to run his AC. He's irritable and exhausted but not unkind. Talking head NPC (Head ID 246). Slightly raspy voice — not cartoonish.

---

## Float — Self-Pity
*Bitter self-pity. Stress the "I".*

`eric1:` Like this town needed any more hard luck. Like *I* needed any more.

## First Greeting
*First greeting.*

`eric2:` Come in already! Close the door behind you! Don't let in the flies!

*At his wit's end — plays if a low-INT PC just grunts back.*

`eric3:` Close the door! Please! Go away and take those damn flies with you!

## Calming Down
*Calming down slightly. Tired complaint.*

`eric4:` Sorry. I'm just a little on edge. All these damn flies and no air conditioning. If I just had a little more power I'd be able to shake 'em... but I don't.

## The Power Quest — Setup
*Explaining the quest. Matter-of-fact.*

`eric5:` There's a power generating station downtown that controls the power to the city. If you could just go there and get permission to give me a little more energy for my air conditioning, I'd be grateful.

*Explaining his biology. Embarrassed understatement.*

`eric6:` They come here because for some odd reason I exude fly nutrients. My body is rich with food for them, and this sweating just makes it worse.

*Weary — he's heard this before.*

`eric7:` I've tried that. I've tried everything. The only thing that keeps the flies off me is not to sweat... and when I don't have enough power to keep cool, I can't.

## Quest Hub
*Neutral hub reply.*

`eric8:` Yes?

*Offering a modest reward. Honest about his limited means.*

`eric9:` I could scrape a little something together for you if you did it. I can't promise much, but then, it's not too huge a favor, either.

*PC accepts the quest. Genuinely moved.*

`eric10:` I can't tell you how grateful I am to you. Please let me know as soon as you're done so I can turn up the air conditioning and take a bath.

*Soft, plaintive.*

`eric11:` Please don't wait too long for this... I'm getting really sick of all these flies.

*Three distinct beats: sarcasm, explanation, expulsion.*

`eric12:` Thanks ever so much for your compassion. That's sarcasm, by the way, in case you hadn't noticed. Get the hell out of my house.

## Where's the Generator?
*Flat contempt for Brian.*

`eric13:` The power generator's on your left as you come into Broken Hills from the outside world. It's run by a guy named Brian, a lazy son of a bitch who does as little work as possible.

## Who Are You?
*Deadpan catalog of losses, each more absurd than the last.*

`eric14:` I'm Eric. I used to run the hotel in town, but I had to sell it because of my fly problem. I also ran the tavern, but had to get out of that, too, for the same reason. Finally, I used to run the generator, but flies kept getting into it.

## Pushing Back on Brian
*Builds from mock-calm to outright demand. "I want my power!" is his loudest moment.*

`eric15:` He said that, did he? Well, you tell him that the generator is uranium powered and that the miniscule amount of energy I require would barely register on the generator. And tell him I want my power!

## Quest Complete — Gratitude
*Flustered with relief.*

`eric16:` You're done with the generator? Thank you so, so much. I can't even begin to describe how grateful I am.

## Alternate Reward — the Power Fist Pills
*Alternative reward offer. Conspiratorial, pleased with himself.*

`eric17:` Francis the mutant has a power fist. He keeps betting it in arm-wrestling contests. The problem is being stronger than him. If you're interested, I can give you some pills that should give you the edge over him.

*Caring instructions, ends warmly.*

`eric18:` These pills are called buffouts. They'll increase your strength, but your mind will suffer for a little bit. Don't take too many or you might get hooked. Good luck with the fist!

## Alternate Quest Entry
*Alternate quest entry. Hopeful, earnest.*

`eric19:` Do you think you can help me with my fly problem? I'd be very grateful.

## Reward Options — Cash
*Pause before the amount — it's everything he has.*

`eric20:` Here's all the money I have... $150. Thank you.

*Touched — repays the kindness with a main quest tip, if the PC declines payment.*

`eric21:` Then I'm doubly grateful. Oh... I hear you're looking for a GECK. Try asking around in NCR. It's to the south.

## Post-Quest
*Genuinely happy.*

`eric22:` Oh yes. The flies have lessened, the air is cool, and I'm less sweaty. Better deal for everyone all around!

---

*End of script*
