# Arthur Pendragon — Voice Actor Script
**Character:** Arthur, son of Uther Pendragon — special random encounter (Brotherhood of Steel knight)
**Total recordable lines:** 29 (**arth1**–**arth29**)

---

> **Direction:** A Monty Python and the Holy Grail parody — Arthur plays a Brotherhood of Steel knight with the exact pompous, dead-serious medieval-knight affect of the film, completely unaware anything about his quest is absurd. Formal, archaic phrasing ("Hail, travelers," "Hast thou seen it?") delivered with total sincerity. Grows visibly exasperated as the player drags out the "Why?" exchange, but never breaks composure into real anger until pushed (theft, the low-INT brush-off). Clipped and business-like once the quest resumes. British RP knight energy throughout — think self-important, slightly harried, and always in character.

> **Important:** Text in [brackets] is staged direction for context only — do not read it aloud.

---

## First Meeting

**arth1:** Hail, travelers. My name is Arthur, son of Uther Pendragon, from the Brotherhood of Steel. We are on quest for the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch. Hast thou seen it?

## The Holy Hand Grenade

**arth2:** The Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch? Why, 'tis only the greatest hand held, anti-personnel weapon of its kind.
**arth3:** We of the Brotherhood of Steel seek to find this most devastating weapon of our ancestors, and secure it from those who would abuse the power of the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch.

## The "Why?" Loop
*Arthur's patience thins with each repeated "Why?"*

**arth4:** Well... Because the Brotherhood of Steel is dedicated to protecting the weak and defenseless, and this thing could really hurt someone.
**arth5:** Umm... Because it's really the only moral thing to do.
**arth6:** Wha... Um... Look now, we're really quite busy. Are you going to help us or not? Have you seen the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch or not?

## Pointing Him Off
*Triggered whether the PC has actually seen it or not — the PC just points somewhere.*

**arth7:** Well, why didn't you just say so? If you could just tell us where, we'll be off and you can get back to whatever it is you were doing.
**arth8:** [Gives the PC 500 caps.] Right then. Thank you for your help. Something for your trouble.

## Claims Not to Have Seen It

**arth9:** Well then, no sense in continuing this conversation. We're off.

## Low-Intelligence PC

**arth10:** Bloody peasant!

## The GECK Side Quest
*PC mentions also questing for the GECK.*

**arth11:** GECK, you said? Why yes, we have two of them. Don't need another one. Thanks all the same though.
**arth12:** No. No. Couldn't do that. Wouldn't be right to end your quest that easily. There's some more of them that way. Go get your own. [He points in a direction about the same way you pointed when you told him where the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch was.]

## Brushing You Off (Talking Again)
*Sequential cycle, not random — plays in order each time the PC tries to talk to Arthur again after finishing the main conversation, looping back to the first line after the fourth.*

**arth13:** No time to talk. We're on quest.
**arth14:** We seek the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch.
**arth15:** For the Brotherhood of Steel!
**arth16:** Hmm... African or European swallow?

## Theft Warnings
*Sequential and escalating — each pickpocket attempt advances to the next, more hostile line.*

**arth17:** Hey now! That's not yours, is it?
**arth18:** I'm warning you. Keep your hands to yourself.
**arth19:** That's it. Enough is enough.

## Trying to Get Your Attention
*Sequential ambient floats, said before the PC has spoken to Arthur at all — loops through all seven before repeating.*

**arth20:** Do you speak the king's tongue?
**arth21:** Hellooo.
**arth22:** Hey, I'm over here.
**arth23:** If you could spare a moment of your time.
**arth24:** Ahem.
**arth25:** Now come on. Don't ignore me.
**arth26:** Pleeeease! I need to ask you something important.

## Departure

**arth27:** Well, we must continue our quest for the Hand Grenade of Antioch. Come, Patsy.

## Second Encounter
*Random encounter with Arthur's group a second time — one plays at random, floater only, no full conversation.*

**arth28:** That was no ordinary rat!
**arth29:** This is exactly why we needed the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch!
