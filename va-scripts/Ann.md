# Ann — VA Script
**Character:** Ann, Connar's wife, Vault Village (formerly Vault City exiles)

**Total recordable lines:** 17 (`annvv1`–`annvv17`)

---

> **Direction:** Ann is a young woman busy cooking for the fledgling Vault Village community. She's warm and grateful early on — genuinely relieved the village survived Vault City's attempts to reclaim them. When she asks the PC for a favor (getting a caravan to trade with the village), she's earnest and a little anxious about the village's food situation. When the PC pushes her for a reward, she gets flustered and a bit wounded — she gives in, but clearly didn't expect to be shaken down. Her later lines shift with the outcome: relieved and thankful if the trade deal worked out, upset and reproachful if the PC killed the merchant or botched the job. The ambient floats (`annvv8`–`annvv14`) are shorter, wary or thankful one-liners said in passing, not full conversation. Female voice, no particular accent — plainspoken, community-minded, protective of her family and neighbors.

---

## First Meeting
*Ann's opening line the first time the PC talks to her.*

`annvv1:` Why hello there, I'm Connar's wife, Ann. You must be the one who helped us out. I can't tell you how much we appreciate all you've done. It's hard to believe that we can finally live in peace without fear of Vault City's goons coming to take us from our homes.

## Asking for Help — Food Shortage
*PC offers to help. Ann explains the village is growing faster than its food supply.*

`annvv2:` Actually, I *could* use some help. Now that we're becoming more organized, we're starting to see our numbers grow. The problem is, I'm not sure we'll have enough food to go around. My husband has attempted to get the caravans to stop here, but they don't' think we're worth their time. I don't suppose you could get them to reconsider?

## Caravan Master Directions
*PC agrees to help. Ann sends the PC to meet the caravan master, Mr. Baron.*

`annvv3:` One of the caravan masters, Mr. Baron, will be passing by here shortly. The caravan trail isn't far from this village. If you could go out there and meet with him, we'd all appreciate it greatly.

## Ready Check
*PC previously said they weren't ready yet. Ann asks again on a later visit.*

`annvv4:` Are you ready to go meet the caravan master yet?

## Trade Deal Secured — Thanks
*PC successfully convinced the merchant to add the village to his trade route.*

`annvv5:` Oh, it's you! Thank you for speaking with Mr. Baron. He had just come by to make trade arrangements with my husband. You've done so much to help us, we're forever in your debt!

## Reward Given — Flustered
*PC pushes Ann for payment instead of accepting thanks. She's caught off guard but gives in.*

`annvv6:` [Ann looks a bit flustered.] Well, yes... alright fine. I had a little something stored away for an emergency, but I suppose you can have it. And to think I saw you as a good person.

## Merchant Killed — Reproach
*PC killed the caravan master instead of negotiating. Ann is upset about the lost trade opportunity.*

`annvv7:` Oh, it's you! I heard what you did to Mr. Baron. I never did care for him, but you didn't have to kill him. We'll stifle as a community without proper trade.

## Ambient Float — Wary of Vault City
*Passing floats said when the PC walks near Ann and the overall village quest hasn't progressed past a certain point. One of the two plays at random.*

`annvv8:` Did Vault City send you?

`annvv9:` We have the right to live here, you know.

## Ambient Float — Food Quest Failed
*Plays if the food/trade quest ended badly (merchant killed or stood up).*

`annvv10:` I have nothing more to say to you.

## Ambient Float — Food Quest Succeeded
*Plays if the food/trade quest was completed successfully.*

`annvv11:` Thanks again for everything!

## Ambient Float — General Thanks
*Later-stage passing floats once the broader village quest has progressed (guns delivered). One of the two plays at random.*

`annvv12:` Thanks so much!

`annvv13:` Finally we are safe.

## Ambient Float — Village Fully Settled
*Plays once the village's diplomatic resolution with Vault City is finished.*

`annvv14:` Thanks for everything! Be sure to stop by again once we expand the place. I'll finally have a working kitchen to cook in.

## Repeat Greeting
*Ann's opening line on any visit after the first.*

`annvv15:` Hello again.

## Checking In — Merchant Status
*PC said they'd handle the merchant meeting later and is now asked how it's going.*

`annvv16:` How did it go?

## Standing Up the Merchant — Angry
*PC never followed through and missed the meeting with the caravan master. Ann is upset — the village lost its shot at a trade deal.*

`annvv17:` What!? You realize this was our last chance to make arragements with him. We look like fools now standing him up. You've doomed us.

*End of script*
