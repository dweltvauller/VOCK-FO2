# Louise — VA Script
**Character:** Lou, owner and madam of the Malamute Saloon, Redding

**Total recordable lines:** 49 (`lou1`–`lou48`, `lou38` shared across {202} and {207})

---

> **Direction:** Lou is the owner and madam of the Malamute Saloon in Redding — a frontier brothel and drinking establishment. She's a tough, no-nonsense woman, older, world-weary, but not cruel. She speaks in a distinctly Southern/rural American drawl: "cain't", "jist", "whattya", "aheard", "perty", "idjit". She has a businesswoman's pragmatism and a genuine, if guarded, protectiveness toward her girls. When she's warm, it comes through reluctantly. When she's cold, she means it. Vocal direction: gravelly, tired, matter-of-fact — a saloon keeper who's seen everything twice. Not shouting, not performative, grounded and dry. Lean into the dialect naturally; don't play it for comedy.
