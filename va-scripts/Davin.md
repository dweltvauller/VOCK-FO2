# Davin — Voice Actor Script
**Character:** Davin, Grisham's son, Modoc slaughterhouse (mcdavin)
**Total recordable lines:** 42 (**davin1**–**davin42**)

---

> **Direction:** Davin is a young man in his twenties, handsome, somewhat sheltered and naive. He works at his father's slaughterhouse in Modoc. He is flirtatious and eager to please, with a slight country-boy charm. His tone shifts between bashful, romantic, and occasionally defensive.

> Lines marked PC are for context only — do not record. Bracketed stage directions (e.g. "[He rips off his clothes...]") are not spoken. [FLOAT] lines are unprompted, short and natural. [WEDDING] lines are part of a scripted ceremony — slightly nervous/excited. [PARTY] lines play when Davin is a traveling companion.
