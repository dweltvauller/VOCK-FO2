# Aldo — VA Script
**Character:** Aldo, Town Greeter of Klamath

**Total recordable lines:** 23 (`aldo1`–`aldo23`)

---

> **Direction:** Middle-aged man, a town-appointed "greeter" who's really just the local drunk working an angle. Wheedling, overly familiar, and a little pathetic — big friendly patter on the surface, but it's a transparent hustle for money to buy more booze ("medicine" for his "dry throat"). Peppered constantly with hiccups and burps; deliver these as genuine involuntary interruptions, not punchlines. Turns petulant and passive-aggressive the moment he's refused a tip, then snaps back to sunny and grateful the instant he's paid. Should never sound truly threatening — his bite is all talk.

---
