# Christopher Wright — VA Script
**Character:** Chris Wright, son of the Wright crime family, New Reno (ncchrwri)

**Total recordable lines:** 42 (`cw1`–`cw47`; gaps at `cw17``cw19``cw27``cw46` — unused tokens. `cw41` is a stage-direction-only beat with no spoken VO — see its entry below)

---

> **Direction:** Young, brash, and a little cocky — a classic mobster's kid who thinks the family name makes him more impressive than he is. He can be genuinely friendly when the PC is on good terms, quickly defensive about his family's honor (especially his dead brother Richard), and outright hostile to slavers. Beneath the swagger there's real grief about Richard's death. Think a loud teenager who's trying very hard to seem dangerous.

> `cw41` is entirely a stage direction (Chris lunges silently) and has no recordable line.
