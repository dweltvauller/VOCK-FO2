> **Direction:** Older adult female, folksy bathhouse madam running the only game in town. Warm and businesslike with a paying customer — she'll upsell the "special baths" with a knowing, slightly ribald wink. But she's openly prejudiced against tribals and quick to write off anyone she's decided isn't worth her time, and that side should land blunt and dismissive, not cartoonishly cruel. No real arc here — she's consistently herself throughout, just noticeably warmer to a good customer and colder to someone already on her bad side.
