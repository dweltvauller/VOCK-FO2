# Hank — VA Script
**Character:** Hank, glowing ghoul reactor worker at the Gecko power plant

**Total recordable lines:** 21 (`hank1`–`hank21`)

---

> **Direction:** Middle-aged-sounding male ghoul, calm and technically-minded — he monitors the reactor core gauges and clearly knows the plant inside and out. Patient and a little wry with "smoothies" (non-ghouls) who don't understand the work, but never hostile. Gets genuinely warm and relieved once the reactor's fixed — pride in a job well done. Turns openly mocking, almost gleeful, if the PC is a low-intelligence "stupid" type wandering into the reactor room; that's the one place his patience runs out.

---
