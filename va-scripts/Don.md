# Don — VA Script
**Character:** Don, slaver boss in charge of the Slaver Camp (near the Den)

**Total recordable lines:** 16 (`don1`–`don16`)

---

> **Direction:** Don runs the Slaver Camp on Metzger's behalf and is exactly as pleasant as that job implies — foul-mouthed, quick to threaten, contemptuous of trespassers. His default register is rough and irritated, always a half-step from violence. He gets genuinely rattled for one beat when the PC calls his bluff about "sparing a few girls" for Metzger — sweat, nervous stammering, a bribe offered too eagerly — before snapping back to menace once he's covered himself. When he signals his men to kill, play it cold and flat, not shouty; he's done this before.

---

## First Approach
*Player walks up. Suspicious, hostile opener.*

`don1:` Hey, what the fuck!? Who the hell are you?

## Player Threatens Him
*Flat challenge, no hesitation.*

`don2:` You first, asshole.

## Player Gives a Nonsense Answer
*He's not amused. Signals his men.*

`don3:` I don't have time for this, get 'em boys.

## Player Says They Want to Join the Slavers
*Dismissive — this isn't a recruiting office. Genuinely annoyed.*

`don4:` Join us? This aint a fuckin recruiting center. If you want to join the slavers go to the Den and speak with Metzger. Now do you need something or are you just here to piss me off?

## Player Says Metzger Sent Them
*Wary, suspicious. Uncomfortable with the idea of more demands.*

`don5:` Metzger sent you? [He fidgets uncomfortably.] Don't tell me he wants more slaves already -- for fuck's sake, we just sent a shipment last week.

## Follow-up: Why Are You Here
*Impatient, wants him to get to the point.*

`don6:` So, why are you here?

## Player Has No Real Business
*Contemptuous. Wants them gone.*

`don7:` Well then get the fuck outta here. You're trespassing and you ain't wanted.

## Calling His Bluff About the Girls
*The bribe moment — nervous, backpedaling, trying to smooth it over. He's caught and knows it.*

`don8:` [Sweat begins to form on the man's tattooed head.] Whoa, hold on a second. I never said I couldn't spare a few. In fact, here, take the key to the shack, that's where we keep our, uh, personal favorites. The pick of the litter, you might say. [He hands you a key and smiles nervously.]

## He Doesn't Buy the "Metzger Sent Me" Story
*Two variants — same beat, played at slightly different failure points of the same lie. Cold, final, signals the kill.*

`don9:` Metzger sent you, huh? Do I look blind to you? You're not even wearing our fuckin mark. I don't know who you are, but you're full of shit and you're wasting my time. For that, you die. [Signals to his men.] Let's waste this loser.

`don10:` Yeah right. You're full of shit and you're wasting my time. For that, you die. [Signals to his men.] Let's waste this loser.

## Returning After Being Told to Leave
*He already ran this player off once. No patience left — straight to violence.*

`don11:` Didn't I tell you to scram? We don't take too kindly to trespassers. Time to die.

## Skeptical of the "Particular Tastes" Story
*He's heard every excuse. Cold threat, no bluster needed.*

`don12:` Bullshit. Metzger's never sent a customer out here before. I don't know what you're trying to pull, but you've got three seconds to get the fuck outta my face before I have my boys pump you full of lead.

## Float — Already Allied With the Slavers
*Ambient greeting instead of full dialogue, once the player has slaver rep. Casual, businesslike.*

`don13:` I'm busy. Go see Metzger in the Den if you want to do a run.

## Float — Combat Taunts
*Plays as he engages. Two variants — plain vs. NCR Ranger PC.*

*Default.*

`don14:` Now you die!

*Player is flagged as an NCR Ranger.*

`don15:` Die Ranger scum!

## Float — Already Has the Shack Key
*Ambient greeting once the player has already gotten the key to the slave shack. Dismissive, wants them out of his hair.*

`don16:` Umm... Give Metzger my regards.
